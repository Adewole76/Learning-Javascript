# Spendy
-On my road to fully understand javascript array manipulation and also object concepts
-Spendy is a small expense management tool used for managing expenses and getting a full overview of how and where you are spending your money the most
-Being a student still being under parental care, my parents demanded an overview of all my expenses to know how i was spending money, but i never could get it from my normal bank app.
-so i built spendy's expense analyzation feature which gives you a detailed analysis of all expenses with a pie chart to add visual representation of where most of my money was going 
-This feature gives you this analysis in a compact and downloadable format.
